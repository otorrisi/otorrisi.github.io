Applied research

  • Consultant Junior Demographer (2020–23)
    Centre for Justice and Accountability & LSE Middle East Centre
    • Screened, organised, validated and analysed data from lists of Yazidi victims of ISIS attacks in Iraq to provide sensible estimates of mortality and information on the demographic profile of killed and kidnapped individuals and families.
  • Research Analyst (2021)
    United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR)
    • Provided assistance on evaluating data typically used to quantify the issue of statelessness, identify pathways and statistical tools to improve current measures and data sources.
  • Research Consultant (2020–2021)
    Italian Consulate in the United Kingdom
    • Designed and led the quantitative section of a research study on trends, demographic characteristics and migration intentions of the Italo-Bangladeshi community in the United Kingdom.
